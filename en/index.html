Why this knee scooter?

  • Air tyres: more comfortable and suitable for outdoors
  • Basket for your things
  • Soft, comfortable knee pad
  • Hand brake with parking position
  • Height adjustable, for people from 1.48 to 1.92 m
  • Maximum user weight 159 kg
  • Foldable: easy to take in the car or on a trip

Is it suitable for me?

You need to be able to bend your knee 90 degrees and stand steadily on your other leg. A knee scooter is not suitable for stairs. If in doubt, ask your doctor or physiotherapist.

Rental terms in short

  1. Price and period. €25 per week, minimum 2 weeks. You can extend per week by email if the knee scooter is not already booked.
  2. Payment. Rent and delivery are paid online after booking. The booking is final once paid. The €40 deposit is paid at handover and refunded when the knee scooter is returned clean and in good condition.
  3. Cancelling. Free up to 24 hours before delivery. If you book for delivery within 24 hours, you can no longer cancel.
  4. Late or early return. Each started extra week counts as a full week. Returning early gives no refund.
  5. Use and damage. Use the knee scooter as intended and do not lend it out. Normal wear is on me; damage from careless use is charged to you. In case of loss, theft or damage beyond repair you pay the new price of €390, minus the deposit.
  6. Risk. You use the knee scooter at your own risk.

Frequently asked questions

How quickly can I get one?

Often within 24 hours. The booking form shows whether a knee scooter is available for your dates.

How do I pay?

Right after booking, online with iDEAL. The €40 deposit is paid at handover, in cash or by payment request.

Can I extend?

Yes, per week, if the knee scooter is not booked afterwards. Email me with the number of extra weeks.

Will my health insurance reimburse the rent?

That depends on your policy. Ask your insurer. I can give you a proof of payment on request, but I cannot guarantee reimbursement.

Do you deliver outside The Hague?

Yes, in all of Zuid-Holland, for example Delft, Leiden, Rijswijk, Zoetermeer and Wassenaar. The fee is €15 + €0.35 per km from The Hague.
